# Test Opinion Summary

Inmate spat on, threatened, and filed a grievance against several other inmates who were housed in Pod 5, a special segregated unit where only one door was supposed to be opened at a time. The Pod 5 inmates broke out and severely injured the first inmate with knives and metal objects. Inmate brings deliberate-indifference claim. No deliberate indifference, says the Eighth Circuit. The officers intervened immediately when the inmate was attacked, and no reasonable jury would find they needed to do more.
