Noah Watson was recently asked for a comment on an Arkansas Democrat-Gazette article about moving from public service to Watson & Watson LLP. Read Noah's full comment :

> It has been a pleasure serving Arkansans for the past 3.5+ years with Attorney General Griffin. I am immensely grateful to the Attorney General for the opportunities he trusted me with, and I will always fondly remember my experiences and coworkers at the Attorney General's office.
> 
> I am excited about this new chapter with new opportunities in private practice at Watson & Watson LLP. We will continue serving Arkansans, with a practice focused on appeals, litigation strategy, constitutional law, election law, and amicus briefing.

The Arkansas Democrat-Gazette's article is available here: [Deputy solicitor general Noah Watson resigns from AG’s office, going to private practice | The Arkansas Democrat-Gazette - Arkansas' Best News Source](https://www.arkansasonline.com/news/2026/sep/24/deputy-solicitor-general-noah-watson-resigns-from/)
